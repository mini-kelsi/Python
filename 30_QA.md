> Create a solution that accepts three integer inputs representing the number of times an employee travel to a job site in a month. Output the total distance traveled to two decimals places according to the miles each employee commutes to the jobsite:

* Employee A : 15.62 miles
* Employee B : 41.85 miles
* Employee C : 32.67 miles

print("Enter Employee A's total trips to the job site:")  
trips_a = int(input())

print("Enter Employee B's total trips to the job site:")  
trips_b = int(input())

print("Enter Employee C's total trips to the job site:")  
trips_c = int(input())

total_miles = (trips_a * 15.62) + (trips_b * 41.85) + (trips_c * 32.67)

print(f"Distance: {total_miles:.2fs} miles")
